## Doubts

- The fetch tool returns summaries from a small model; Bright Outlook, growth and openings values were not read verbatim from the pages. Re-verify any number before it goes in the final document.
- BLS OOH pages for sheet metal workers, elevator installers, construction supervisors, telecom installers and security and fire alarm installers returned 403, so BLS figures for those come from O*NET (which uses BLS projections) only.
- The Energy Engineers and Energy Auditors counts on O*NET (166,700 and 153,900) look like parent-group figures; Energy Auditors equals the BLS Construction and Building Inspectors figure exactly.
- NEC 90.2(B) exclusions (ships, rail rolling stock, aircraft, automobiles, utility generation and substations) were stated from knowledge, not from a page I opened; confirm against the code text.
- NFPA 70E page, ASME A17.1 page, OSHA wind and solar pages did not load; the standards for wind, solar and elevators lean on search-result articles and O*NET. OSHA 1910.147, .332 and .333 were opened.
- 1910.147 itself excludes electrical-utilization installations (covered by 1910.333), so 'LOTO' for electrical workers is really 1910.333 plus NFPA 70E.
- The skill mapping to the app's 13 skills is my judgement; I did not inspect the app's skill definitions.
- The document says 24 occupations but lists 18 adjacent entries; the other six (core) were not part of this check.
- Standards listed without an opened source (UL, TIA, SMACNA, ASHRAE, IEEE, GWO, 1910.268, 1910.269, 1926 Subpart K, EPA 608) come from general knowledge.
